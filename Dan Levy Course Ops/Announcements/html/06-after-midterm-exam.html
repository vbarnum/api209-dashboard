Instructions for accessing your API-209 Midterm Exam - Stage 1

  1. Navigate to https://www.gradescope.com
  2. Click Log In in the upper right corner of the page
  3. Choose School Credentials in the lower left corner of the box
  4. Choose Harvard Key
  5. Authenticate with Harvard Key
  6. From there you can click on API-209-2025 to access the course
  7. Then click API-209-2025 Midterm Stage 1 to access your Stage 1 exam and score
  8. To view comments on your exam, click the Download Graded Copy button on the bottom of the screen to download a copy of your exam with attached comments.
  9. If you are unable to see API-209-2025 listed, please email Victoria Barnum (victoria_barnum@hks.harvard.edu)
