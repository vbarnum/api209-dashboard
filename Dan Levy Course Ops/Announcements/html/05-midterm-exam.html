Important Information for the API-209 Midterm Exam

A — ESSENTIAL INFO

(1) The exam will be held on Friday, Oct 10th, 8:00 am–10:00 am, L140.

(2) The exam will be a two-stage exam, where the first part will be individual, and the second part will be in groups. Details in syllabus and FAQs below.

B — PREPARING FOR THE EXAM

(1) Material to help you prepare: To help you prepare for the exam, we have posted on our Canvas site the files below.

  • Past exams with and without solutions: My advice is that you take these exams in exam-like conditions after you have studied for the exam.
  • Solutions to online quizzes from online modules
  • Practice Problems: These are basic practice problems on the mechanics of doing statistical inference.
  • Solutions to problem sets 1 and 2.
  • Your graded problem sets (in Canvas only): Please note that in Canvas you can see not only the grades of your problem sets but also (and more importantly) the feedback written by the CAs for you. Here are instructions on how to make sure you access this information.

(2) Extra Office Hours: Our teaching team will be offering extra office hours to help you prepare for the exam. No sign-up needed for any of the office hours. The schedule of office hours is here, and will be updated over the next few days. Please note that this schedule is different than the one for regular office hours, and that some of the original office hours were rescheduled to better accommodate the exam schedule.

(3) What to bring to the exam:

  • A calculator: No phones allowed. A simple calculator will do as long as it has a way of calculating square roots.
  • Cheat sheets: You can bring two double-sided sheets of paper
    • First sheet: I recommend that one sheet include two big summary hypothesis tests tables (from handout 8). These tables can be annotated.
    • Second sheet: Include whatever you want to write.
  • Your Harvard ID

(4) Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs): We have prepared a document (available at http://bit.ly/209faqs) with a list of frequently asked questions about the exam, so you can all benefit from the answers to the questions that your classmates ask. We will be updating this document as questions come.

C — LOGISTICS OF THE EXAM

  • On the day of the exam, please arrive 15 minutes early to settle and make sure you are ready to begin on time.
  • In terms of timing, you will have 80 minutes for the first stage (individual) and 35 minutes for the second stage (group). There will be a short break in between the two stages in which you will learn who your Stage 2 team members are.

Hope this is helpful. As always, please do not hesitate to contact me or any member of the API-209 teaching team if you have any questions or need further information. Above all, remember to focus on your learning.
